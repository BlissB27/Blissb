# Product

## Register

brand

(Overall site default — home, category pages, and marketing sections are storefront/persuasion-driven. The checkout flow specifically is treated as `product` register per-task: forms, multi-step flow, functional over persuasive.)

## Users

Home bakers' customers in and around Braselton, GA looking to order handmade cookies, cakes, and desserts for pickup, local delivery, or nationwide shipping. Also corporate/catering customers ordering in bulk for events.

## Product Purpose

Bliss-B Desserts' e-commerce storefront: browse small-batch cookies/cakes/desserts, build a cart, and check out via pickup, local delivery, or nationwide shipping — with Stripe handling payment. Success = a smooth path from browsing to a completed, correctly-priced order with minimal friction or confusion at checkout.

## Brand Personality

Artisanal and warm — handmade, small-batch, approachable. Not corporate, not cold-luxury. Warm brown/cream palette, serif+sans type pairing (Bricolage Grotesque + Inter), real product photography.

## Anti-references

None specified by the user. Default: avoid generic SaaS-template look, avoid corporate coldness, avoid anything that undercuts the "handmade in a real kitchen" feel.

## Design Principles

- Warmth over slickness — real photos, brand-brown accents, rounded/soft shapes over sharp corporate edges.
- Functional clarity in transactional flows (cart, checkout) — this is where register shifts toward `product`: fewer distractions, clear steps, accurate pricing shown at every point.
- Reuse the established brand tokens (brand-bg/brand-brown/brand-text/brand-muted/brand-border/brand-accent) rather than introducing new ad-hoc colors.
- No invented urgency or fake scarcity — real stock, real delivery windows, real pricing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the reasonable default for a public e-commerce site: sufficient contrast, keyboard operability, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No additional user-specific needs specified.
